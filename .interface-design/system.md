# EVENT CREW interface system

## Direction

Production board for an event producer checking a staffing partner between calls. The surface should feel like a crew list prepared before doors open: direct, controlled and specific to on-site work.

## Tokens

- Stage black `#0A0A09`
- Work paper `#F2EFE6`
- Tape yellow `#F4BD00`
- Rig grey `#66645D`
- Steel line `#CAC7BD`
- Signal red `#B83A2D`, errors only

Depth uses surface-color shifts and quiet dividers. Do not add decorative shadows or gradients. Spacing uses a 4 px base. Page gutters are 20–56 px.

## Hierarchy

Roboto Condensed carries short display headlines. Manrope carries body, navigation, labels and controls. Display type is tight and sentence case. Utility labels may use uppercase with tracking. The primary CTA and hero headline are the focal elements.

## Signature

The 6 px yellow crew line extends the mark in the logo. Use it in five places: hero, section headings, services roster, process timeline and contact brief. Yellow marks an action or reading route and stays below ten percent of visible area.

## Controls

- Primary link/button: 48 px minimum height, square 2 px radius, stage-black text on tape-yellow.
- Text input/select/textarea: 48 px minimum height, inset work-paper surface, 1 px steel-line border, 3 px yellow focus ring.
- Header/mobile controls: 44 px minimum hit area; preserve Escape, focus trap, inert background and focus return.
